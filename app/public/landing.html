Where educators come together to explore what’s next.

The NJ AI Literacy Summit is a free, full-day professional learning event that brings educators together to share innovative ideas, exchange practices, and learn from each other in the age of AI.

The schedule of sessions is driven entirely by educator input. Every session is proposed and led by practitioners who are doing incredible work in their schools and classrooms every day.

Whether you’re just beginning to explore AI or you’ve been building with it for years, the NJ AI Literacy Summit is designed to meet you where you are and make sure you leave knowing more than when you walked in.

Come curious. Leave inspired.
